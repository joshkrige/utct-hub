    Rules that carry a penalty

    Six things. Breaking one costs him 30 minutes or the race.

    If something goes wrong

    Emergency

    MYSOS app, or the emergency number printed on his race number. Both are on him.

    If he's missing

    Don't go looking on the trail. Tell the station captain at the aid station he was due at, and watch the tracker at ultratrailcapetown.com.

    Before you believe anything dramatic

    Most ultra problems are one of four things, in this order: too little sodium, too little food, going too hard early, getting cold.

    Medical

    Shivering plus confusion or slurred speech is medical — get the station captain, don't send him on. If he can't keep water down for over an hour, that's the station medic's call, not yours.
